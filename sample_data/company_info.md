# CloudSecure Solutions - Company Profile

## Industry
**SaaS / Cloud Security**

## Company Description
CloudSecure Solutions is a B2B SaaS company that provides cloud infrastructure security and compliance monitoring tools for mid-market enterprises. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in San Francisco, the company helps organizations automate their security posture management across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform.

CloudSecure's flagship product, **GuardianCloud**, continuously monitors cloud environments for misconfigurations, compliance violations, and security threats. The platform provides real-time alerts, automated remediation workflows, and comprehensive compliance reporting for standards including SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS.

The company serves over 500 customers globally, ranging from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 companies, and has achieved significant recognition in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Cloud Security Posture Management.
