# CloudSecure Solutions - Compliance & Certifications

## 1. SOC 2 Type II

### 1.1 Certification Details
- **Standard**: SOC 2 Type II (Trust Services Criteria)
- **Audit Period**: Annual (July 1 - June 30)
- **Auditor**: Deloitte & Touche LLP
- **Last Audit**: July 2024
- **Next Audit**: July 2025

### 1.2 Trust Services Criteria
Our SOC 2 report covers all five Trust Services Criteria:
- **Security**: Protection against unauthorized access
- **Availability**: System availability for operation and use
- **Processing Integrity**: Complete, valid, accurate, timely processing
- **Confidentiality**: Designated confidential information is protected
- **Privacy**: Personal information is collected, used, retained, and disposed of properly

### 1.3 Report Availability
- SOC 2 Type II reports available under NDA
- Bridge letters provided for periods between audits
- Report covers GuardianCloud platform and supporting infrastructure

## 2. ISO 27001:2022

### 2.1 Certification Scope
- Information Security Management System (ISMS) for GuardianCloud platform
- Development, operations, and support services
- All CloudSecure Solutions employees and contractors

### 2.2 Certification Body
- **Certifier**: British Standards Institution (BSI)
- **Certificate Number**: IS 123456
- **Issue Date**: March 15, 2023
- **Expiration Date**: March 14, 2026
- **Recertification Audit**: Annual surveillance audits

### 2.3 Statement of Applicability
Our ISMS covers all ISO 27001:2022 Annex A controls with the following exclusions:
- A.7.13 - Equipment disposal (handled by AWS)
- A.8.9 - Configuration management (handled via automated IaC)
